## general overview of the project
Develop a web application named 'News Reader' that allows users to read and stay updated on the latest news articles from various sources. The application offers a clean, user-friendly interface and personalized news feeds based on user interests.

## software functional requirements
1. **User Registration**
   - The system shall provide a Registration Page where users can create an account by entering a username and password.

2. **User Login**
   - The system shall provide a Login Page where users can log in to their account by entering their username and password.

3. **News Browsing**
   - The system shall allow users to browse different categories of news, such as politics, sports, and entertainment, on the Dashboard Page.

4. **Search Functionality**
   - The system shall enable users to search for specific topics or keywords to find relevant articles.

5. **Article Preview**
   - The system shall display the headline, summary, and source of each article in the news feed.

6. **Article Details**
   - The system shall allow users to click on an article to read the full text on the Article Details Page.

7. **User Logout**
   - The system shall provide a logout functionality that returns users to the Login Page.
