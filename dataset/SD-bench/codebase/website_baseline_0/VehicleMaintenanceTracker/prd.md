## general overview of the project
Develop a web application named 'Vehicle Maintenance Tracker' that helps users manage and track the maintenance schedule and history of their vehicles.

## software functional requirements
1. **User Registration**
   - The system shall provide a Registration Page where users can create an account by entering a username and password.

2. **User Login**
   - The system shall provide a Login Page where users can log in to their account by entering their username and password.

3. **Vehicle Information Input**
   - The system shall allow users to input vehicle information, including make, model, year, and mileage.

4. **Maintenance Task Tracking**
   - The system shall enable users to track regular maintenance tasks, such as oil changes, tire rotations, and brake inspections.

5. **Reminders and Notifications**
   - The system shall send reminders and notifications to users for upcoming maintenance tasks based on predefined intervals or mileage.

6. **Maintenance History Viewing**
   - The system shall allow users to view the maintenance history of their vehicles.

7. **Maintenance Record Management**
   - The system shall provide functionality for users to update or delete maintenance records.

8. **User Logout**
   - The system shall allow users to log out of their account, returning them to the Login Page.
