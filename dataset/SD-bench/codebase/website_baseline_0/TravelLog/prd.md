## general overview of the project
Develop a web application named 'Travel_Log' that acts as a digital travel journal, allowing users to document and share their travel experiences.

## software functional requirements
1. Users shall be able to register for an account on the Registration Page by entering a username and password.
2. Users shall be able to log in to their account on the Login Page by entering a username and password.
3. Users shall be able to create and save travel journal entries, including details such as destination, dates, activities, photos, and personal reflections.
4. Users shall be able to view and organize past entries by destination, date, or category.
5. Users shall be able to edit or delete their travel entries.
6. Users shall be able to share selected travel entries with friends and family through a shareable link or directly on the platform.
7. Users shall be able to search for specific entries or destinations.
8. Users shall be able to log out of their account, returning to the Login Page.
