## general overview of the project
Develop a web application named 'Trip Saver' that helps users find the most cost-effective and time-efficient mode of transportation for their trips.

## software functional requirements
1. **User Registration**
   - The system shall provide a Registration Page where users can create an account by entering a username and password.

2. **User Login**
   - The system shall provide a Login Page where users can log in to their account by entering their username and password.

3. **Trip Details Input**
   - The system shall allow users to input trip details, including starting point, destination, and travel date.

4. **Transportation Suggestions**
   - The system shall provide suggestions for the most cost-effective and time-efficient transportation options, including public transport, ride-sharing, and walking.

5. **Cost and Time Estimates**
   - The system shall display the estimated costs and travel times for each transportation option.

6. **Comparison of Transportation Options**
   - The system shall enable users to compare different transportation options based on distance, cost, and time.

7. **Saving Preferred Options**
   - The system shall allow users to save their preferred transportation options for future reference.

8. **User Logout**
   - The system shall provide a logout functionality that returns users to the Login Page.
