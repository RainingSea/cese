## general overview of the project
Develop a web application named 'Travel Memories Sharer' that allows users to share their travel memories with friends and family by creating interactive and visually appealing travel albums.

## software functional requirements
1. **User Registration**
   - The system shall provide a Registration Page where users can register for an account by entering a username and password.

2. **User Login**
   - The system shall provide a Login Page where users can log in to their account by entering their username and password.

3. **Album Creation and Upload**
   - The system shall allow users to create and upload travel albums.

4. **Album Customization**
   - The system shall enable users to customize the layout and design of their travel albums.

5. **Album Sharing**
   - The system shall allow users to share their albums with friends and family, with options for public or private sharing.

6. **Album Exploration**
   - The system shall provide functionality for users to explore and view albums shared by other users.

7. **User Interaction**
   - The system shall allow users to interact with other users by liking, commenting, or sharing their albums.

8. **User Following**
   - The system shall enable users to follow other users and receive updates on their new albums or content.

9. **User Logout**
   - The system shall provide a logout functionality that returns users to the Login Page.
