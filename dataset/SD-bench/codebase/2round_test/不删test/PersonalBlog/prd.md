## general overview of the project
The objective of the 'PersonalBlog' web application is to provide users with a simple platform to create, manage, and share their personal blog entries without relying on a database; all data will be stored locally in text files.

## software functional requirements
1. Users shall be able to log in to their account by entering a username and password on the Login Page.
2. Users shall be able to register a new account by providing a username, password, and email on the Registration Page.
3. Users shall be able to view a list of their blog posts on the Main Blog Page, with options to create a new post or view existing posts.
4. Users shall be able to create a new blog post by providing a title and content on the New Post Page.
5. Users shall be able to view the full content of a selected post on the View Post Page.
6. Users shall be able to edit an existing post by modifying the title and content on the Edit Post Page.
7. Users shall be able to delete a blog post from the View Post Page.
8. Users shall be able to navigate back to the main blog page from the View Post, Edit Post, and New Post Pages using a back button.
9. Users shall be able to log out of their account from the Main Blog Page.
10. Users shall be shown appropriate messages for successful or unsuccessful actions (login, registration, post creation, etc.) on each relevant page.
