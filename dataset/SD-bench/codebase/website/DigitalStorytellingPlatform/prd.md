## general overview of the project
Develop a comprehensive web application for a Digital Storytelling Platform that enables users to create, edit, and share their stories without the need for SQL databases.

## software functional requirements
1. Users shall be able to log in using their username and password on the login page, which will redirect them to the Story Creation Page upon successful login.
2. Users shall be able to navigate to the registration page to create a new account.
3. Users shall be able to create a new account by entering a username, password, and email on the registration page.
4. Users shall be able to create a new story by entering a story title and content on the story creation page.
5. Users shall be able to save their story by clicking the "Save Story" button on the story creation page.
6. Users shall be able to edit the story title and content in the provided input fields on the story creation page.
