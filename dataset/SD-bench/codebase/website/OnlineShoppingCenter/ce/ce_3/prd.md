## general overview of the project
The task is to develop an online shopping center web application that allows users to browse products, manage their shopping carts, and process checkout procedures without using SQL for data storage.

## software functional requirements
1. **User Login**
   - The system shall provide a Login Page where users can enter their username and password to log in.

2. **User Registration**
   - The system shall provide a Registration Page where users can register a new account by providing a username, password, and email.

3. **Product Browsing**
   - The system shall provide a Product Listing Page where users can browse a list of products.
   - Each product shall have an option to add it to the shopping cart.

4. **Shopping Cart Management**
   - The system shall provide a Shopping Cart Page where users can view the contents of their shopping cart.
   - Users shall be able to remove items from their shopping cart on the Shopping Cart Page.

5. **Checkout Process**
   - The system shall allow users to proceed to checkout by clicking the Checkout button on the Shopping Cart Page.
   - The Checkout Page shall enable users to enter their shipping address and payment information.
   - Users shall be able to confirm and finalize their order on the Checkout Page.

6. **Order Confirmation**
   - The system shall provide an Order Confirmation Page that displays an order summary detailing the user's order.
   - Users shall be able to navigate back to the Product Listing Page from the Order Confirmation Page to continue shopping.
