## general overview of the project
Age Calculator is an application that calculates a person’s age based on their birthdate. Users can input their birthdate, and the app will display their age in years, months, and days. It can also provide the number of days remaining until their next birthday.

## software functional requirements
1. The system shall allow users to input their birthdate through a user-friendly interface.
2. The system shall calculate and display the user's age in years, months, and days based on the inputted birthdate.
3. The system shall calculate and display the number of days remaining until the user's next birthday.
