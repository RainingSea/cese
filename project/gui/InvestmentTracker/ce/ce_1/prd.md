## general overview of the project
Investment Tracker is a finance software application that helps users track and manage their investment portfolios. Users can input their investments, including stocks, bonds, mutual funds, and other assets, and categorize them into different categories such as growth, income, and balanced portfolios. The application will provide visualizations and reports that show users the performance of their investments over time. 

## software functional requirements
1. The system shall allow users to input investment details, including but not limited to stocks, bonds, mutual funds, and other assets.

2. The system shall enable users to categorize their investments into different portfolios, such as growth, income, and balanced.

3. The system shall provide visualizations that display the performance of investments over time.

4. The system shall generate reports that summarize investment performance.

5. The system shall allow users to set investment goals.
