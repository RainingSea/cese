- **Problem 3:** Lack of functionalities around managing tutoring requests.

  - Adding a status field for tutoring requests to track whether they are "pending", "completed", or "canceled".

- **Data Structures:** Enhance the `User` and `TutoringRequest` models by adding methods to:
  - Validate inputs (e.g., checking email format).
  - Manage passwords (though not explicitly concerning security here, having a placeholder for encryption or strong storage could be beneficial).
