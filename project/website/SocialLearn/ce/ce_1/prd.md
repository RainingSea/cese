## general overview of the project
Develop a web application 'SocialLearn' that connects individuals with a passion for learning, allowing them to create profiles, share educational resources, and engage with like-minded learners.

## software functional requirements
1. **User Registration**: The system shall allow users to register for an account by entering a username and password on the Registration Page.
2. **User Login**: The system shall allow users to log in to their account by entering their username and password on the Login Page.
3. **Profile Management**: The system shall allow users to create and update their profile, including specifying their areas of interest or expertise.
4. **Study Group Participation**: The system shall allow users to join study groups or communities based on their learning objectives.
5. **Resource Sharing**: The system shall allow users to share and access educational resources such as articles and courses.
6. **Messaging**: The system shall allow users to message others within study groups or forums.
7. **User Logout**: The system shall allow users to log out of their account, returning them to the Login Page.
