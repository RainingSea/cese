The Great Gatsby|F. Scott Fitzgerald|A story of wealth and love in the Jazz Age|The novel chronicles an era that Fitzgerald himself dubbed the "Jazz Age". Following the shock and chaos of World War I, American society enjoyed unprecedented levels of prosperity during the "roaring" 1920s as the economy soared.
To Kill a Mockingbird|Harper Lee|A story of racial injustice in the American South|Set in the small Southern town of Maycomb, Alabama, during the Depression, To Kill a Mockingbird follows three years in the life of 8-year-old Scout Finch, her brother, Jem, and their father, Atticus.
1984|George Orwell|A dystopian novel about totalitarianism|The story takes place in an imagined future, the year 1984, when much of the world has fallen victim to perpetual war, omnipresent government surveillance, historical negationism, and propaganda.
