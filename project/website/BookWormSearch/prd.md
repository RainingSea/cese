## general overview of the project
Develop a web application named 'BookWorm Search' that enables users to search for books in their personal libraries or online with ease. The application will provide a simple interface for efficient book search and management.

## software functional requirements
1. **User Registration**
   - The system shall provide a Registration Page where users can register for an account by entering a username and password.

2. **User Login**
   - The system shall provide a Login Page where users can log in to their account by entering their username and password.

3. **Book Search**
   - The system shall provide a search bar on the Dashboard Page that allows users to search for books by entering the title, author, or keywords.

4. **View Search Results**
   - The system shall display a list of matching results with relevant details such as title, author, and summary after a search is performed.

5. **Book Details**
   - The system shall allow users to click on a book from the search results to view detailed information on the Book Details Page, including a description and author details.

6. **Add to Reading List**
   - The system shall provide an 'Add to Reading List' button on the Book Details Page that allows users to add books to their personal reading list.

7. **Manage Reading List**
   - The system shall provide a dedicated Reading List Page where users can view and manage their reading list.

8. **User Logout**
   - The system shall allow users to log out of their account, returning them to the Login Page.
