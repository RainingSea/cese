## general overview of the project
Develop a web application named 'News Reader' that allows users to read and stay updated on the latest news articles from various sources. The application offers a clean, user-friendly interface and personalized news feeds based on user interests.

## software functional requirements
1. Users shall be able to register for an account on the Registration Page by entering a username and password.
2. Users shall be able to log in to their account on the Login Page by entering a username and password.
3. Users shall be able to browse different categories of news, such as politics, sports, and entertainment, on the Dashboard Page.
4. Users shall be able to search for specific topics or keywords to find relevant articles.
5. Users shall be able to view the headline, summary, and source of each article in the news feed.
6. Users shall be able to click on an article to read the full text on the Article Details Page.
7. Users shall be able to log out of their account, returning to the Login Page.
