## general overview of the project
Develop a web application named 'QuickSearch' that allows users to quickly search for specific words or phrases within a collection of reference materials and books. The application provides an easy way to access relevant information from books.

## software functional requirements
1. Users shall be able to register for an account on the Registration Page by entering a username and password.
2. Users shall be able to log in to their account on the Login Page by entering a username and password.
3. Users shall be able to search for specific words or phrases within the collection of books by entering them in a search bar on the Dashboard Page.
4. Users shall be able to view a list of matching results with relevant book details such as title, author, summary, and cover image.
5. Users shall be able to click on a book to view more information, including a detailed description and relevant context on the Book Details Page.
6. Users shall be able to add books to their personal reading list by clicking an 'Add to Reading List' button on the Book Details Page.
7. Users shall be able to view and manage their reading list on a dedicated Reading List Page.
8. Users shall be able to log out of their account, returning to the Login Page.
