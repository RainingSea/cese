## general overview of the project
Develop a web application named 'QuickSearch' that allows users to quickly search for specific words or phrases within a collection of reference materials and books. The application provides an easy way to access relevant information from books.

## software functional requirements
1. **User Registration**
   - The system shall provide a Registration Page where users can create an account by entering a username and password.

2. **User Login**
   - The system shall provide a Login Page where users can log in to their account by entering their username and password.

3. **Search Functionality**
   - The system shall provide a search bar on the Dashboard Page that allows users to enter specific words or phrases to search within the collection of books.

4. **Display Search Results**
   - The system shall display a list of matching results that includes relevant book details such as title, author, summary, and cover image.

5. **Book Details View**
   - The system shall allow users to click on a book from the search results to view more information on the Book Details Page, including a detailed description and relevant context.

6. **Add to Reading List**
   - The system shall provide an 'Add to Reading List' button on the Book Details Page that allows users to add books to their personal reading list.

7. **Manage Reading List**
   - The system shall provide a dedicated Reading List Page where users can view and manage their personal reading list.

8. **User Logout**
   - The system shall provide a logout functionality that allows users to log out of their account, returning them to the Login Page.
