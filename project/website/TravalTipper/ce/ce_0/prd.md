## general overview of the project
Develop a web application named 'Travel_Tipper' that provides users with helpful travel tips and advice based on their destination and travel preferences.

## software functional requirements
1. **User Registration**: The system shall allow users to register for an account by entering a username and password on the Registration Page.
2. **User Login**: The system shall allow users to log in to their account by entering their username and password on the Login Page.
3. **Travel Details Input**: The system shall allow users to input their travel details, including destination, trip duration, and interests.
4. **Personalized Travel Tips**: The system shall generate and display recommendations based on the user's travel details, including local customs, safety tips, transportation options, cultural etiquette, and popular attractions.
5. **Search Functionality**: The system shall allow users to search for tips related to specific destinations or travel interests.
6. **Favorite Tips Saving**: The system shall allow users to save their favorite travel tips for easy access.
7. **User Logout**: The system shall allow users to log out of their account, returning them to the Login Page.
