Develop a web application 'CulturalStoryteller' that allows users to explore and discover stories from various cultures, promoting cultural appreciation and diversity through the power of storytelling.

## software functional requirements
1. **User Registration**
   - The system shall provide a Registration Page where users can create an account by entering a username and password.

2. **User Login**
   - The system shall provide a Login Page where users can log in to their account by entering their username and password.

3. **Story Exploration**
   - The system shall provide a Dashboard Page that allows users to explore a collection of stories from different cultures.

4. **Story Details**
   - The system shall provide a Story Details Page where users can click on a story to read its full text and view details about the story's cultural background.

5. **Story Search**
   - The system shall allow users to search for stories using keywords, cultural origins, or categories.

6. **Bookmarking Stories**
   - The system shall allow users to bookmark stories by clicking an 'Add to Bookmarks' button on the Story Details Page.

7. **Manage Bookmarks**
   - The system shall provide a dedicated Bookmarks Page where users can view and manage their list of bookmarked stories.

8. **User Logout**
   - The system shall allow users to log out of their account, returning them to the Login Page.
