## general overview of the project
Develop a web application named 'The Cultural Calendar' that provides users with a curated collection of cultural events, festivals, and celebrations from around the world. The application allows users to explore event details and set reminders for events they are interested in.

## software functional requirements
1. **User Registration**
   - The system shall provide a Registration Page where users can register for an account by entering a username and password.

2. **User Login**
   - The system shall provide a Login Page where users can log in to their account by entering their username and password.

3. **View Upcoming Events**
   - The system shall display a calendar or list of upcoming cultural events on the Dashboard Page.

4. **Event Details**
   - The system shall allow users to click on an event to view detailed information, including the significance, history, and location of the event, on the Event Details Page.

5. **Search Events**
   - The system shall provide a search functionality that allows users to search for events by keywords, categories, or dates.

6. **Set Reminders**
   - The system shall allow users to add events to their personal list of reminders by clicking a 'Set Reminder' button on the Event Details Page.

7. **Manage Reminders**
   - The system shall provide a dedicated Reminders Page where users can view and manage their list of reminders.

8. **User Logout**
   - The system shall allow users to log out of their account, returning to the Login Page.
