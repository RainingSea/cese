  - **Suggestions:** Implement checks when reading from the text files to handle cases where the files are missing or corrupted. Additionally, provide feedback messages to users for invalid inputs during registration and login.

- **Problem 3:** Limited data retrieval functionalities.
  - **Suggestions:** Enhance the `Event` and `Reminder` classes with methods for searching (finding by name) and filtering events/reminders based on criteria such as date or significance. This can enhance the user experience by allowing quick access to relevant information.
