## general overview of the project
Develop a web application named 'Sports Equipment Inventory Tracker' that helps sports teams and organizations efficiently manage their equipment inventory. The application allows users to track, update, and organize various types of sports equipment.

## software functional requirements
1. **User Registration**:  
   - The system shall allow users to register for an account by entering a username and password on the Registration Page.  

2. **User Login**:  
   - The system shall allow users to log in to their account by entering a username and password on the Login Page.  

3. **Equipment Management**:  
   - The system shall allow users to input and update information about different types of equipment (e.g., jerseys, balls, training gear) on the Dashboard Page.  

4. **Equipment Details View**:  
   - The system shall display the quantity, condition, availability, and location of each equipment item.  

5. **Alerts for Maintenance/Replacement**:  
   - The system shall allow users to set alerts for maintenance or replacement of equipment items.  

6. **Equipment Search**:  
   - The system shall allow users to search for specific equipment by name, type, or location.  

7. **Equipment Filtering**:  
   - The system shall allow users to filter the equipment list based on criteria such as condition or availability.  

8. **User Logout**:  
   - The system shall allow users to log out of their account, returning them to the Login Page.
