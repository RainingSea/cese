## general overview of the project
Develop a web application named 'Wishlist Tracker' that helps users keep track of the items they want to purchase in the future.

## software functional requirements
1. **User Registration**
   - The system shall provide a Registration Page where users can create an account by entering a username and password.

2. **User Login**
   - The system shall provide a Login Page where users can log in to their account by entering their username and password.

3. **Add Items to Wishlist**
   - The system shall allow users to add items to their wishlist on the Dashboard Page by entering the item name, description, and desired price.

4. **View Wishlist**
   - The system shall enable users to view their wishlist, displaying item details such as name, description, and price.

5. **Update Wishlist Items**
   - The system shall allow users to update the details of items in their wishlist.

6. **Remove Items from Wishlist**
   - The system shall enable users to remove items from their wishlist.

7. **User Logout**
   - The system shall provide a logout functionality that returns users to the Login Page.
