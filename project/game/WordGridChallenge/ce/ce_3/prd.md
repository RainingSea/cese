## general overview of the project
Develop a Word Grid Challenge game. You must design a GUI.

## software functional requirements
1. The system shall present a grid of letters to the player.
2. The player shall be able to find hidden words by connecting adjacent letters horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
3. The system shall calculate and display the player's score based on the words formed.
4. The game shall include multiple levels with increasing difficulty, which may involve:
   - Larger grids.
   - More complex word lists.
   - Additional letter patterns.
5. The system shall implement a timer that tracks the time taken to find all the words in the grid.
6. The player shall be challenged to complete the grid as quickly as possible.
7. The application shall be developed using Python and the Pygame library.
8. Data related to the game, including word lists and scores, shall be stored in local text files.
