## general overview of the project
Develop a Word Grid Challenge game. You must design a GUI.

## software functional requirements
1. The system shall provide a grid of letters for the player to interact with.
2. The system shall allow the player to find hidden words by connecting adjacent letters horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
3. The system shall calculate and display the player's score based on the words formed.
4. The system shall include multiple levels with increasing difficulty, which may involve larger grids, more complex word lists, and additional letter patterns.
5. The system shall implement a timer that tracks the time taken by the player to find all the words in the grid.
6. The system shall store game data, including word lists and player scores, in local text files.
