## general overview of the project
Develop a Number Connect game. You must design a GUI.

## software functional requirements
1. The game shall allow the player to connect numbers in a sequence to form a continuous path.
2. The game board shall consist of a grid of numbered tiles, and the player must connect the tiles in numerical order, starting from the first number.
3. The player shall be able to move horizontally or vertically to the adjacent tiles, with diagonal movements not allowed.
4. The path formed by the player shall be a continuous line without breaking, and the player shall not be able to revisit any tile once it has been used.
5. The game shall offer multiple levels with increasing difficulty, where the grid size and number of tiles increase.
6. The game shall include a timer to challenge players to complete the path within a certain time.
