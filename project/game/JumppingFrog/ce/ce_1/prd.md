## general overview of the project
Develop a Jumping Frog game. You must design a GUI.

## software functional requirements
1. The system shall allow the player to control a frog that must jump between platforms positioned at various heights on the screen.
2. The system shall enable the player to move the frog left or right using the arrow keys or WASD keys.
3. The system shall allow the player to initiate a jump by pressing a designated jump button (spacebar or another key), with the ability to control the jump direction and distance.
4. The system shall implement moving platforms that can move horizontally or vertically, requiring the player to time their jumps to land on them.
5. The system shall end the game if the frog falls into the water, requiring the player to restart from the beginning.
6. The system shall track the player's score, incrementing it with each successful landing on a platform.
7. The system shall include a timer that tracks how long the player can keep the frog jumping without falling into the water.
