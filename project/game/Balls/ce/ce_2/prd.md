## general overview of the project
Develop a Battle of Balls Game

## software functional requirements
1. The game shall display the player's ball at the center of the screen.
2. The game shall allow the player to move their ball using the up, down, left, and right arrow keys.
3. The game shall detect collisions between the player's ball and smaller enemy balls.
4. Upon collision with a smaller enemy ball, the player's ball shall grow in size, and the smaller ball shall be consumed.
5. The game shall end if the player's ball is consumed by a larger enemy ball.
6. The game shall initialize the player's ball and four enemy balls at the start of the game.
7. The player's ball shall be larger than the enemy balls at the beginning of the game.
8. The game shall spawn enemy balls (non-player) on the map, ensuring they are smaller than the player's ball.
