## general overview of the project
Shape Shifter is a puzzle game where players have to rearrange geometric shapes to match a target pattern. Players are given a set of shapes, including squares, triangles, and circles, and they need to rotate and position the shapes correctly to form the desired pattern.

## software functional requirements
1. The system shall allow players to select geometric shapes from a predefined set, including squares, triangles, and circles.
2. The system shall enable players to rotate the selected shapes to achieve the correct orientation.
3. The system shall allow players to position the shapes on the game board to match the target pattern.
4. The system shall verify the arrangement of shapes against the target pattern and provide feedback to the player indicating whether the arrangement is correct or not.
5. The system shall provide an option for players to reset the puzzle at any time, clearing the current arrangement of shapes.
