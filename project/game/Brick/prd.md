## general overview of the project
Develop a Brick Breaker game.

## software functional requirements
1. The player must be able to control a paddle using the left and right arrow keys on the keyboard.
2. The ball must bounce off the paddle, walls, and bricks within the game environment.
3. Each brick must start with 3 lives. When hit by the ball, the brick must split into two smaller bricks, each having 1 less life.
4. When a brick’s life reaches 0, it must disappear from the game.
5. The game must start when the player presses either the left or right arrow key.
6. Bricks must be arranged at the top of the window, while the paddle must be positioned at the bottom of the window.
7. The ball must be launched from the center of the window and move upwards to hit the bricks.
