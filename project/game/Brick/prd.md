## general overview of the project
Develop a Brick Breaker game.

## software functional requirements
1. The player must be able to control a paddle using the left and right arrow keys.
2. The ball must bounce off the paddle, walls, and bricks.
3. Each brick must start with 3 lives.
4. When a brick is hit by the ball, it must split into two smaller bricks, each with 1 less life.
5. When a brick’s life reaches 0, it must disappear from the game.
6. The game must start once the player presses the left or right arrow key.
7. Bricks must be arranged at the top of the window, while the paddle must be placed at the bottom.
8. The ball must be launched from the center of the window and move upwards to hit the bricks.
