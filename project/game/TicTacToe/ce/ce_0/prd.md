## general overview of the project
Develop a Tic-Tac-Toe game. You must design a GUI.

## software functional requirements
1. The system shall provide a 3x3 grid interface for players to place their symbols ("X" or "O").
2. The system shall allow players to take turns placing their symbols in empty cells of the grid.
3. The system shall check for a winning condition after each move, determining if a player has formed a continuous line of three matching symbols either horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
4. The system shall declare a winner if a player successfully places three matching symbols in a row.
5. The system shall detect and declare a draw if the grid is filled and no player has formed a line of three matching symbols.
6. The system shall provide feedback at the end of the game, indicating whether player "X" won, player "O" won, or if the game ended in a draw.
7. The system shall allow players to restart the game after a match is over.
8. The system shall include a timer that tracks and displays the duration of the match.
