## general overview of the project
Develop a Flappy Bird Clone. You must design a GUI.

## software functional requirements
1. The system shall allow the player to control a bird that constantly falls due to gravity.
2. The system shall enable the player to make the bird flap by clicking the mouse or pressing any key on the keyboard to move the bird upward.
3. The system shall generate a series of pipes that appear from the right side of the screen, which the player must navigate through.
4. The system shall ensure that the pipes are spaced vertically, creating a gap that the bird must pass through without touching the pipes.
5. The system shall move the pipes from right to left across the screen.
6. The system shall implement a scoring system that awards the player one point each time the bird successfully passes through a set of pipes.
7. The system shall increase the difficulty of the game by making the pipes move faster as the player scores more points.
8. The system shall end the game when the bird collides with a pipe or falls to the ground, at which point the final score shall be displayed.
9. The system shall provide the player with the option to restart the game at any time after the game ends to attempt to beat their high score.
