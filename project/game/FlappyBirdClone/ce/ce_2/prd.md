## general overview of the project
Develop a Flappy Bird Clone. You must design a GUI.

## software functional requirements
1. The system shall allow the player to control a bird that constantly falls due to gravity.
2. The system shall enable the player to make the bird flap by clicking the mouse or pressing any key on the keyboard to move the bird upward.
3. The system shall generate a series of pipes that appear from the right side of the screen, spaced vertically.
4. The system shall detect collisions between the bird and the pipes, ensuring the player must avoid hitting them.
5. The system shall move the pipes from right to left across the screen.
6. The system shall provide a gap between the pipes for the bird to pass through.
7. The system shall implement a scoring system where the player earns one point each time the bird successfully passes through a set of pipes.
8. The system shall increase the difficulty of the game by making the pipes move faster as the player scores more points.
9. The system shall end the game when the bird collides with a pipe or falls to the ground, displaying the final score to the player.
10. The system shall allow the player to restart the game at any time after the game ends to attempt to beat their high score.
