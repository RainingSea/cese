## general overview of the project
Develop a Memory Game. You must design a GUI.

## software functional requirements
1. The system shall present a grid of face-down cards at the start of the game.
2. The system shall ensure that each card has a matching pair and that the cards are shuffled randomly at the start of the game.
3. The player shall be able to flip two cards at a time by clicking on them with the mouse.
4. The system shall check if the two flipped cards are a match.
   - If the cards match, they shall remain face-up.
   - If the cards do not match, they shall be turned face-down again after a brief delay.
5. The player shall continue flipping cards until all pairs have been matched and the board is cleared.
6. The system shall include a timer that tracks the time taken to find all matching pairs.
7. The player shall be able to restart the game at any time, which will reshuffle the cards and reset the timer.
8. The system shall include a scoring system that awards points based on the time taken to finish the game, with faster completions earning higher scores.
