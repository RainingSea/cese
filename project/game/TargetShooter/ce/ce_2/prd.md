## general overview of the project
Develop a Target Shooter game. You must design a GUI.

## software functional requirements
1. The system shall allow the player to control a shooter using the mouse to aim and click to shoot.
2. The system shall display moving targets that appear at random locations on the screen.
3. The system shall require players to shoot the targets as accurately as possible within a specified time limit.
4. The system shall calculate and display points earned based on the accuracy and speed of shooting the targets, awarding higher scores for quicker and more accurate hits.
5. The system shall include a timer that counts down, indicating the remaining time for the player to hit as many targets as possible.
6. The system shall provide an option for the player to restart the game after completing a round to improve their performance.
7. The system shall feature different levels with increasing difficulty, such as faster-moving targets or a greater number of targets appearing simultaneously.
8. The system shall maintain a leaderboard that tracks the highest scores achieved, allowing players to compete for the top spot.
