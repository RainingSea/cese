## general overview of the project
Develop a Dodge the Falling Blocks game. You must design a GUI.

## software functional requirements
1. The game shall allow the player to control a small character positioned at the bottom of the screen using the left and right arrow keys for movement.
2. The game shall generate falling blocks from the top of the screen that the player must avoid. If the player collides with a block, the game shall end immediately.
3. The game shall randomly spawn falling blocks that move straight down the screen. The speed of the falling blocks shall increase as the player survives longer.
4. The game shall implement a scoring system that tracks the amount of time the player has survived. The score shall increase the longer the player avoids the falling blocks.
5. The player shall be restricted to horizontal movement only; vertical movement of the character shall not be allowed.
6. Upon collision with a falling block, the game shall end and display the player's final score on the screen.
