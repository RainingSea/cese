## general overview of the project
Design a Single-Player Tank Battle Game

## software functional requirements
1. The game shall be played on a 20x20 grid with no visible grid lines.
2. The player's tank shall be represented in yellow color.
3. Enemy tanks shall be represented in silver color.
4. Obstacles shall be represented in brown color.
5. The player shall be able to move the tank using the arrow keys.
6. The player shall be able to fire bullets using the 'Enter' key.
7. There shall be two fixed enemy tanks that can shoot in four directions: up, down, left, and right.
8. Both the player and enemy tanks shall have health points, starting at 200 for each.
9. When the player's tank is hit by a bullet, it shall lose 10 health points.
10. When an enemy tank is hit by a bullet, it shall lose 100 health points.
11. If the health of the player's tank reaches zero, the tank shall be destroyed.
12. If the health of an enemy tank reaches zero, the enemy tank shall be destroyed.
13. Destroying an enemy tank shall earn the player 200 points.
14. The game shall end when either the player’s tank is destroyed or all enemy tanks are destroyed.
15. Upon game end, the player's score shall be displayed.
