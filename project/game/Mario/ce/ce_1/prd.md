## general overview of the project
Develop a Super Mario game.

## software functional requirements
1. Mario Movement:
   - Mario shall be able to move left using the left arrow key.
   - Mario shall be able to move right using the right arrow key.
   - Mario shall be able to jump using the up arrow key.

2. Block Interaction:
   - When Mario hits a block, a mushroom shall appear above the block.

3. Mushroom Behavior:
   - Mushrooms shall move left and fall to the ground after being released from a block.
   - If Mario touches a mushroom, the mushroom shall disappear and increase the score by 1000.

4. Enemy Behavior:
   - Enemies shall move left and right randomly.
   - If Mario touches an enemy, the game shall register a loss for Mario.

5. Game Objective:
   - Mario's goal shall be to reach the flagpole at the end of the game.
   - When Mario touches the flagpole, the game shall end.

6. Game Settings:
   - There shall be a ground where Mario and enemies can stand and walk.
   - A single block shall appear above Mario's starting position, which releases a mushroom when hit.

7. Scoring System:
   - The score shall increase by 1 every second.
   - The score shall increase by 100 when Mario hits a block.
   - The score shall increase by 1000 when Mario touches a mushroom.
   - The score shall increase by 10000 when Mario reaches the flagpole.
