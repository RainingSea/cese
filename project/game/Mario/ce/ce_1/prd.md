## general overview of the project
Develop a Super Mario game.

## software functional requirements
1. **Character Movement**
   - Mario shall be able to move left and right using the left and right arrow keys.
   - Mario shall be able to jump using the up arrow key.

2. **Block Interaction**
   - When Mario hits a block, a mushroom shall appear above the block.
   - The block shall be interactive and respond to Mario's action.

3. **Mushroom Behavior**
   - The mushroom shall move left and fall to the ground after being released from the block.
   - If Mario touches the mushroom, it shall disappear and add to the player's score.

4. **Enemy Behavior**
   - Enemies shall move left and right randomly within the game environment.
   - If Mario touches an enemy, the game shall register a loss for Mario.

5. **Game Objective**
   - Mario's goal shall be to reach the flagpole at the end of the game.
   - When Mario touches the flagpole, the game shall end.

6. **Game Settings**
   - The game shall include a ground area where Mario and enemies can stand and walk.
   - A single block shall appear above Mario's starting position, which can be hit to release a mushroom.

7. **Scoring System**
   - The score shall increase by 1 every second.
   - The score shall increase by 100 when Mario hits a block.
   - The score shall increase by 1000 when Mario touches a mushroom.
   - The score shall increase by 10000 when Mario reaches the flagpole.

8. **Data Storage**
   - Game data, including scores and settings, shall be stored in local text files.
