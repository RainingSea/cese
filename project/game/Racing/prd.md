## general overview of the project
Develop a racing game.

## software functional requirements
1. The game interface shall feature a three-lane route.
2. There shall be obstacles on each lane, but not all three lanes will have obstacles simultaneously.
3. The three lanes at the starting point shall be free of obstacles.
4. The vehicle's speed and the distance traveled shall be displayed in the top right corner of the interface.
5. The player shall be able to control the car using the arrow keys:
   - Up arrow key to speed up.
   - Down arrow key to slow down.
   - Left and right arrow keys to shift to an adjacent lane.
6. The vehicle shall remain stationary in the game interface while the obstacles move backward, simulating the vehicle's forward movement.
7. When the "s" key is pressed, the car shall stop (set the speed to 0).
8. There shall be two types of obstacles:
   - One type that causes the vehicle to slow down upon contact.
   - Another type that immediately ends the game upon contact.
