## general overview of the project
Develop a racing game.

## software functional requirements
1. The game interface shall feature a three-lane route.
2. The game shall display a few obstacles on each lane, but not all three lanes will have obstacles simultaneously.
3. At the starting point, all three lanes shall be free of obstacles.
4. The game shall display the vehicle's speed and the distance traveled in the top right corner of the interface.
5. The player shall be able to control the car using the arrow keys:
   - Up arrow key to speed up.
   - Down arrow key to slow down.
   - Left arrow key to shift to the left lane.
   - Right arrow key to shift to the right lane.
6. The vehicle shall remain stationary while the obstacles move backward, simulating the vehicle's forward movement.
7. When the "s" key is pressed, the car shall stop, setting the speed to 0.
8. There shall be two types of obstacles:
   - One type that causes the vehicle to slow down upon contact.
   - Another type that immediately ends the game upon contact.
9. Data shall be stored in local text files.
