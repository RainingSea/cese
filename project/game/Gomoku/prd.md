## general overview of the project
Develop a basic two-player Gomoku game.

## software functional requirements
1. **Board Setup**
   - The game board must be visually represented with grid squares formed by black intersecting vertical and horizontal lines.
   - The background color of the board must be orange yellow.

2. **Player Assignment**
   - Players must be assigned pieces of either black or white color for the game.

3. **Piece Placement**
   - Both players must use the left mouse button to place their respective pieces on the board.

4. **Game Conclusion**
   - The game must detect when one player achieves victory.
   - Upon victory, the game must prevent further piece placements on the board.
   - The game must display the winning player's information on the board.
