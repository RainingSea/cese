## general overview of the project
Develop a Slide Maze Challenge game. You must design a GUI.

## software functional requirements
1. The system shall allow the player to navigate through a maze by sliding tiles horizontally or vertically to reveal paths.
2. The system shall provide an objective for the player to move the player character from the starting point to the exit tile, while avoiding obstacles and traps.
3. The system shall consist of a maze composed of tiles that can be moved to change the layout, enabling the player to create a path.
4. The system shall include multiple levels with increasing difficulty, featuring more complex mazes and additional obstacles such as traps or moving barriers.
5. The system shall implement a timer that tracks the time taken to solve each maze, providing a challenge for the player to complete the puzzle quickly.
6. The system shall allow players to earn bonus points by collecting stars scattered throughout the maze, which will be placed in hard-to-reach areas.
7. The system shall provide an option for players to reset the maze and try again or choose a different level.
