## general overview of the project
Develop a Maze Runner game. You must design a GUI.

## software functional requirements
1. The system shall allow the player to control a character using keyboard arrow keys (up, down, left, right) to navigate through the maze.
2. The system shall provide a maze filled with obstacles that the player must avoid while finding the correct path to the exit.
3. The system shall allow the player to collect stars scattered throughout the maze, earning bonus points for each star collected.
4. The system shall include multiple levels with increasing difficulty, such as larger mazes, more complex paths, and additional obstacles.
5. The system shall require players to strategically plan their movements to avoid dead ends and reach the exit within the shortest time possible.
6. The system shall implement a timer that tracks the time it takes to complete each level, allowing the player to try to improve their time with each attempt.
7. The system shall allow players to track their progress and completion times across different levels.
8. The system shall feature a scoring system based on completion time, stars collected, and the number of moves made to reach the exit.
