## general overview of the project
Develop a Treasure Hunt game. You must design a GUI.

## software functional requirements
1. The game shall provide a maze environment where the player can control a character to navigate and find hidden treasure.
2. The maze shall consist of walls and paths, with the treasure placed at a random location in each level.
3. The player shall be able to move the character up, down, left, or right using the arrow keys or WASD keys.
4. The game shall track the player's score, which increases with each successful treasure find.
5. Upon finding the treasure, the player shall be transported to a new maze that is more complex and includes additional obstacles.
6. The game shall impose a time limit for each level, and it shall end if the player does not find the treasure within the allotted time.
7. The game shall include a timer that tracks the duration taken by the player to find the treasure, and it shall save the player's best time.
8. The player shall have the option to restart the game after completing a level or upon losing the game.
